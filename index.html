05HELP CENTRE

Questions before
you get started?

Here are a few helpful points to consider before sending an enquiry.

Ask us directly ↗
What details should I share for a calibration enquiry?+

Share the instrument name, make/model if available, measurement range, quantity, location and any required standard or deadline. The team can advise what additional details are needed.

Can I enquire about a testing requirement?+

Yes. Include the sample type, parameters you need tested, quantity, purpose and any required method or reporting format. The laboratory must confirm whether the requested test is within its available scope.

How can I confirm accreditation or certification?+

Ask the laboratory for its current certificate and approved scope. Check that the specific instrument or test parameter is covered before relying on an accreditation claim.

How long will a service take and what will it cost?+

Time and pricing depend on the instrument or sample, scope, quantity and service conditions. Share your requirement to request a direct confirmation.

Where is Khushi Calibration located?+

The lab is identified as being in the Rasulgarh area of Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Please confirm the exact address and visit timing with the team before travelling.
